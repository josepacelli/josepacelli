## About

Senior Systems Analyst and Software Engineer with 10+ years of experience translating business needs into reliable, scalable digital solutions. My background combines software engineering, IT infrastructure, databases, and project delivery, supported by a degree in Electronic Engineering and an MBA in Project Management from Universidade de Fortaleza (UNIFOR).

I work across the full delivery lifecycle: designing APIs and backend services with Java, Spring Boot, Python, and FastAPI; building maintainable interfaces with React and Angular; and operating solutions across PostgreSQL, MySQL, Docker, Linux, and Windows. I bring engineering rigor, pragmatic problem solving, and agile delivery practices to products that need to create measurable value.
